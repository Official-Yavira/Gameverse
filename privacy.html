Privacy Notice

Draft version: 26 November 2026 · Document version: privacy-2026-11-26-v5

This draft describes the main data flows currently used by GAMEVERSE. It is not the final privacy notice. The controller identity, contact channel, retention periods and final processor/transfer details must be completed before paid orders open.

Controller: Lorenzo Lisi, individual (natural person), operating under the GAMEVERSE brand. GAMEVERSE is not a separate incorporated company.

Planned privacy contact: gameverse.ink@proton.me (must be created and tested before launch).

The complete contact address and a working privacy contact channel must be supplied before paid orders open.

1. Data we process

  • Account information: username, email address, account identifier and authentication/session information.
  • Purchase and entitlement information: game purchased, Stripe Checkout session identifier, amount, currency and purchase date.
  • Consent evidence: the account identifier, game identifier, timestamp, language/locale, consent choices and versions of the Terms, Privacy Notice and Refund Policy that were shown.
  • Download operations: information needed to verify ownership and generate a short-lived secure download link.

Payment card details are entered into Stripe Checkout. GAMEVERSE does not intend to store full card numbers or security codes in its own database.

2. Why we use the data

We use this information to create and secure accounts, process purchases, confirm payment, provide access to purchased downloads, document legally required consent, prevent fraud and abuse, respond to support requests, and maintain accounting and transaction records where required by law. We do not describe this as marketing consent and do not use the purchase consent checkbox for newsletters.

3. Service providers

GAMEVERSE currently uses Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage, and Stripe for payment checkout and payment processing. These providers process information to provide their services and under their applicable privacy/security terms. Stripe receives information required to process the payment. Supabase hosts the GAMEVERSE project in its EU West region. Further details about international processing and safeguards must be checked against the final provider agreements before launch.

4. Retention

Account data is retained while the account is active and as needed to provide the service. Purchase, consent and financial records may be retained for the periods required to comply with accounting, tax, dispute-resolution and legal obligations. Other data is deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed. The precise retention schedule remains to be finalized before launch.

5. International transfers

Because GAMEVERSE is intended to serve customers worldwide and uses third-party providers, some data may be processed in countries outside the customer's country of residence. The final notice will identify applicable transfer safeguards and the relevant provider information before sales open.

6. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Requests must be sent to the public privacy contact that will be published before launch. Some transaction records may need to be retained where the law requires it.

7. Cookies and marketing

The final notice must identify any analytics, advertising, cookies or similar technologies used on the live site. No marketing subscription or newsletter consent is collected through the game-purchase consent form.

8. Updates

Material changes to this notice will be published with a new version number. The version shown to a customer at checkout is retained with the consent record for that purchase.
