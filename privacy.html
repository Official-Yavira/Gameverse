Privacy Notice

Updated: 27 November 2026 · Document version: privacy-2026-11-27-v10

This Privacy Notice explains how personal data is processed when you visit GAMEVERSE, create or use an account, purchase a game, contact support or download a game. It describes the services and integrations currently present on the website and may be updated if those services change.

Privacy contact: gameverse.ink@proton.me.

1. Personal data we process

  • Account data: email address, username, account identifier, password/authentication data and session information needed to register, sign in and secure an account.
  • Purchase and entitlement data: game identifier, Stripe Checkout Session identifier, payment status, amount, currency, transaction date and the entitlement that enables a purchased game to appear in MY GAMES.
  • Billing data: the name and Swiss billing address entered before checkout. These details are sent to Stripe and associated with the Stripe Customer record used for Checkout. GAMEVERSE's purchase and consent tables do not store the full billing address or payment-card details.
  • Consent records: account and game identifiers, timestamp, locale, consent choices and the version numbers of the Terms of Sale, Privacy Notice and Refund Policy shown at checkout.
  • Download data: information used to check whether an account is entitled to a game and to generate a time-limited secure download link.
  • Technical and security data: service providers may process IP address, browser and device information, timestamps, request details and security logs when delivering the website, authentication, checkout, advertising or file downloads. GAMEVERSE also keeps a minimal server-side rate-limit record for authenticated checkout and download requests: account identifier, action type, time-window start and request counter. These counters are not available to anonymous or ordinary authenticated database clients and are used to reduce automated abuse.
  • Support correspondence: the sender and recipient email addresses, message content, attachments and information you choose to include when contacting support.

Payment-card numbers and security codes are collected by Stripe Checkout. GAMEVERSE does not receive or store full card numbers or card security codes.

2. Purposes of processing

Data is used to operate and secure GAMEVERSE accounts; authenticate customers; validate the billing address for the initial Switzerland-only sales area; prepare and verify payments; record purchase consent; provide purchased downloads; apply account-based request limits to checkout and downloads; prevent fraud and misuse; answer support requests; maintain accounting and transaction records; and comply with applicable legal obligations. The purchase form does not subscribe customers to a newsletter or use purchase consent as marketing consent.

Some checks are automated, including account/email confirmation, Swiss billing-country validation, payment-status verification and the check that an account owns a game before a download is issued. These checks determine whether checkout or download access can proceed. If you believe a check has produced an error, contact support so it can be reviewed.

4. International processing and safeguards

The main Supabase project region is Ireland; Supabase Pte. Ltd. is based in Singapore and Supabase states that processing by it or its subprocessors may take place in other countries. Its published Data Processing Addendum incorporates Standard Contractual Clauses and a Swiss addendum for transfers where applicable. Stripe states that data may be transferred to Stripe, LLC in the United States and to Stripe affiliates and subprocessors in other jurisdictions; its Data Transfers Addendum describes the transfer mechanisms used for Switzerland, including the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework and contractual clauses where applicable. GitHub and Google are US-based providers and may process information in the United States and other countries in which they and their subprocessors operate. Proton Mail's stated server locations include Switzerland, Germany and Norway. The esm.sh module is delivered through internationally distributed network infrastructure, so the location of an individual request may vary.

Where applicable law requires safeguards for a transfer to a country without an adequate level of data protection, the relevant provider's data-processing terms and recognised transfer mechanisms are used where available. Provider locations, subprocessors and transfer arrangements can change; the linked provider notices and contractual documents contain their current details. The Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner explains the rules for transfers of personal data abroad.

5. Retention

Account and profile information is kept while the account is active and for as long as needed to operate and secure the service. If an account is closed or deletion is requested, information is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed, except for data that must be kept for security, fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations. Purchase records and supporting accounting documents that form part of the required accounting records are retained for the ten-year period required by Swiss law, calculated from the end of the relevant financial year. Consent evidence is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to document the transaction and establish or defend legal claims. Technical logs held by service providers are retained according to their applicable security and retention policies.

6. Your rights and contact

Under applicable Swiss data-protection law, you may request information about personal data concerning you and, where the legal conditions are met, request correction, deletion or destruction, restriction of certain processing, or data portability. You may also exercise other rights available under applicable law and may lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. Send requests to gameverse.ink@proton.me. Some purchase and accounting records cannot be deleted while a statutory retention obligation applies.

7. Browser storage, cookies and advertising choices

The authentication system uses browser storage to maintain the GAMEVERSE sign-in session. The website also includes the Google AdSense publisher script. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies when advertising features are enabled. The use of such technologies and personalised advertising is subject to applicable law, the choices presented through the consent mechanism configured for the website and Google's publisher policies. Google requires publishers to use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework when serving personalised ads to users in Switzerland, the EEA or the United Kingdom. GAMEVERSE will not intentionally enable personalised advertising in those regions without the required consent setup. You can also review Google's ad settings.

No newsletter or direct-marketing subscription is collected through the purchase form. This notice will be updated if additional analytics, advertising, tracking or consent-management tools are added.

8. Changes to this Notice

Material changes will be published with a new document version and update date. The version shown at checkout is recorded with the customer's purchase-consent record.
