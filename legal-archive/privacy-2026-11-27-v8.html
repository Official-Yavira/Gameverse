Privacy Notice

Updated: 27 November 2026 · Document version: privacy-2026-11-27-v8

This notice explains the main personal-data processing activities connected with GAMEVERSE accounts, digital purchases, downloads and support. It remains subject to final pre-launch review and may be updated as the service changes.

Data controller and contact
Controller: Lorenzo Lisi, individual (natural person), operating under the GAMEVERSE brand. GAMEVERSE is not a separate incorporated company.
Postal contact address: Via Cantonale 85, 6936 Cademario, Switzerland.
Privacy contact: gameverse.ink@proton.me.

1. Data we process

  • Account information: username, email address, account identifier and authentication/session information.
  • Purchase and entitlement information: game purchased, Stripe Checkout session identifier, amount, currency and purchase date.
  • Billing information: name and Swiss billing address (street, postal code and city) supplied before checkout and passed to Stripe to prefill and complete the payment. GAMEVERSE uses the country and postal-code format for the initial Switzerland-only sales check; the full address is processed by Stripe as the payment provider.
  • Consent evidence: the account identifier, game identifier, timestamp, language/locale, consent choices and versions of the Terms, Privacy Notice and Refund Policy that were shown.
  • Download operations: information needed to verify ownership and generate a short-lived secure download link.

Payment card details and the billing address are processed by Stripe Checkout. GAMEVERSE does not intend to store full card numbers or security codes in its own database. The billing address is associated with the Stripe Customer record used to prefill Checkout; it is not added to the GAMEVERSE purchase database.

2. Why we use the data

We use this information to create and secure accounts, process purchases, check whether the supplied billing address meets the initial Switzerland-only sales requirement, confirm payment, provide access to purchased downloads, document legally required consent, prevent fraud and abuse, respond to support requests, and maintain accounting and transaction records where required by law. We do not describe this as marketing consent and do not use the purchase consent checkbox for newsletters.

3. Service providers

GAMEVERSE uses the following providers to operate the service:

  • Supabase provides account authentication, the database and private file storage. The GAMEVERSE project is hosted in Supabase's EU West region (Ireland). Supabase acts as a service provider/processor under its Data Processing Addendum.
  • Stripe provides hosted checkout and payment processing. Stripe receives the information needed to complete and verify a payment, including customer and billing details. Depending on the processing activity, Stripe may act as a processor or as an independent controller for its own legal, security and fraud-prevention purposes.
  • Proton Mail provides the support mailbox used for customer enquiries. Messages sent to support may contain account, order or contact details.

GAMEVERSE does not store full payment-card numbers or security codes. Payment credentials are collected by Stripe Checkout.

4. Retention

Account and profile information is retained while the account remains active. If an account is closed or deletion is requested, the account data is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed, except for information that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling or legal obligations. Purchase records and supporting accounting documents are retained for ten years from the end of the relevant financial year where they form part of the accounting or tax records required under Swiss law. Consent evidence linked to a purchase is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to document the transaction and defend or establish legal claims. Temporary technical logs are kept only for the period needed for security and troubleshooting.

5. International transfers

The GAMEVERSE Supabase project is hosted in Ireland. Supabase Pte. Ltd is established in Singapore; its Data Processing Addendum includes Standard Contractual Clauses and a Swiss addendum for transfers where applicable. Stripe states that it may transfer personal data to Stripe, LLC in the United States and to affiliates and subprocessors in other jurisdictions. Stripe's Data Processing Agreement and Data Transfers Addendum provide the applicable transfer mechanisms, including adapted Standard Contractual Clauses for Switzerland where required. Proton Mail is provided by Proton AG in Switzerland; its own privacy and subprocessor information applies to support correspondence. Information about international processing and safeguards is also available in the providers' current privacy notices and data-processing terms.

6. Your rights

Under applicable data-protection law, you may request access to your personal data and, where the legal requirements are met, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability or object to certain processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority. Send requests to gameverse.ink@proton.me. Some purchase and accounting records cannot be deleted during the statutory retention period.

7. Cookies and marketing

The account system uses browser storage to maintain authentication and session state. As of this notice's update date, the storefront does not load third-party analytics or advertising scripts; the advertising area visible on the site is a placeholder and does not itself set advertising cookies. No newsletter subscription or marketing consent is collected through the game-purchase form. This section will be updated if analytics, advertising or other tracking technologies are added.

8. Updates

Material changes to this notice will be published with a new version number. The version shown to a customer at checkout is retained with the consent record for that purchase.
