1. General approach

GAMEVERSE does not offer automatic refunds for a change of mind after purchase and immediate digital supply. Refunds outside mandatory legal rights are considered only as exceptional requests at GAMEVERSE's discretion.

2. Seven-day exceptional-request window

Customers may ask GAMEVERSE to consider an exceptional refund within 7 calendar days from the purchase date. This is a voluntary review window, not a replacement for or limitation of any statutory rights that apply for a longer period.

3. Examples of exceptional cases

  • The same purchase was charged more than once.
  • Payment was taken but the game was not made available in MY GAMES.
  • The download file is damaged or cannot be downloaded.
  • A serious technical fault prevents the game from being used and GAMEVERSE cannot resolve it within a reasonable time.

4. How requests are reviewed

Requests are reviewed individually. GAMEVERSE may ask for the order email, approximate purchase date and a description of the problem so that the transaction can be identified and investigated. If a voluntary refund is approved, it is sent to the original payment method through the payment provider.

5. Mandatory consumer rights

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or delays a consumer's mandatory rights or remedies under the laws that apply to the purchase, including rights relating to non-delivery, defective or non-conforming digital content, or any statutory withdrawal right that has not validly been lost.
