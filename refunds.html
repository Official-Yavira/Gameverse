1. Online purchases and change of mind

Swiss law does not provide a general statutory right to revoke an ordinary purchase solely because it was made online. GAMEVERSE does not promise an automatic refund for a change of mind after an order has been placed. This does not affect any mandatory right or remedy that applies to non-delivery, a defective or non-conforming product, or another issue that cannot lawfully be excluded.

2. Voluntary seven-day request window

As a voluntary customer-service measure, a customer may ask GAMEVERSE to consider an exceptional refund within 7 calendar days from the purchase date. Each request is reviewed individually. This is a goodwill review window, not a statutory withdrawal period and not a guarantee that a refund will be granted.

3. Examples of issues we will review

  • The same purchase appears to have been charged more than once.
  • Payment was taken but the game was not added to MY GAMES after Stripe confirmed payment.
  • The download file is damaged or cannot be downloaded.
  • A serious technical problem prevents the game from being used and cannot be resolved within a reasonable time.

4. How to request help or a refund

Contact gameverse.ink@proton.me using the email address associated with your GAMEVERSE account. Include your approximate purchase date, the game name and a description of the problem. Do not send your password, full card number or card security code. GAMEVERSE may ask for additional information reasonably needed to identify the order and review the request. If a voluntary refund is approved, it will be sent to the original payment method through Stripe.

5. Billing address outside Switzerland

The initial sales area is limited to Swiss billing addresses. If Stripe reports a billing country other than Switzerland for a paid Checkout Session, GAMEVERSE will not grant access to the game and will initiate a refund to the original payment method. A temporary charge may appear before the refund is completed.

6. Mandatory rights

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or delays a mandatory right or remedy that a customer has under the law applicable to the purchase. If the game is not delivered or does not conform to the contract, contact GAMEVERSE so the issue can be investigated and handled under the applicable rules.
